# SMILE: Student Monitoring for Inclusive Learning Education

Project spec. SMILE is built first for SNEd (SPED) classes.

Status: planning. Source of truth for what to build and in what order. Database design lives in `schema.sql`.

> The original spec was based on general knowledge of DepEd policy. Owner-supplied IEP, curriculum, and approved non-graded SF9 references now supplement it; see `docs/IEP_REFERENCE.md` and `docs/CURRICULUM_REPORT_REFERENCE.md`. Confirm unresolved rules and form adaptations with the school or division. Items needing confirmation are listed in section 10.

---

## 1. Purpose

A web app that helps a SNEd (SPED) teacher:
1. Keep each learner's record (SMILE ID, optional LRN, disability info, guardians, health, documents)
2. Track progress against each learner's IEP
3. Run the class day to day (attendance, behavior, health measurements, what needs attention)
4. Print the reports DepEd, the school, and parents ask for

It is a **school-side working tool**. DepEd's LIS stays the official registry. This tool stores the LRN and enrollment status, and exports data for LIS encoding. It is never the official enrollment record.

## 2. Users and roles

The **teacher is the main user.** In a one-teacher school there is no SPED coordinator, so the teacher holds both the teacher and school admin roles.

| Role | Who (first release) | Scope |
|---|---|---|
| `system_admin` | The developer | Technical settings, new schools, backups. No routine access to learner data. Any access is logged. |
| `school_admin` | The SNEd teacher (later: principal/coordinator) | Sections, accounts, level-change approvals, audit log, school settings |
| `sped_teacher` | The SNEd teacher | Own section: learners, IEPs, progress, attendance, health, reports |
| `regular_teacher` | Later | View-only accommodations and progress summary for mainstreamed learners |
| `parent` | Later | Own child only |
| `principal` (optional) | Only if requested | Read-only dashboards and reports, plus sign-off on level changes |

- One user can hold several roles through Spatie's `roles` and `model_has_roles` tables. Use `spatie/laravel-permission`.
- The adviser is a teacher assigned to a section (`sections.adviser_id`), not a separate role.
- An adviser sees only the learners in their own section(s).

## 3. Key domain rules

- **SMILE ID** (`smile_id`): the tool's own learner ID. It is generated by SMILE, not typed in and not taken from the school's student number. It is globally unique, permanent, never reused, random (not built from name, birthdate, or LRN), and shown on every printed report. Format: `SM-` plus 8 characters, for example `SM-7K3QX9PD`. SMILE does not use the school's student ID.
- **LRN**: DepEd's 12-digit Learner Reference Number. **Optional.** Unique when present, permanent, issued through the LIS. The tool stores it and does not generate it. It can be empty while a learner waits for approval or has none yet (`lrn_status`). A learner without an LRN is still fully identified by the SMILE ID. `lrn_status = 'issued'` requires an LRN.
- **Duplicates without an LRN:** when adding a learner with no LRN, warn (never block) if the same name and birthdate already exist in the school.
- **Non-graded learners**: SNEd learners are non-graded and are re-enrolled every year in a non-graded class. `grade_level` stays empty until a formal change.
- **Enrollment with pending documents (owner-confirmed):** learners may enroll during the ongoing school year even when supporting documents or the SPED assessment document are missing. Track missing items for follow-up rather than blocking non-graded enrollment. The teacher may assess through observed manifestations and teacher-conducted tests; medical/doctor documentation is supporting evidence when available. Documents accepted by the teacher may be submitted later, before the school year ends. Teacher observations are not medical diagnoses. Parental consent must already be obtained before saving learner data, but the signed copy may follow later. Phase 1 must record the teacher's confirmation of existing consent; this does not require every attachment before enrollment.
- **Assessment record separation:** Phase 1 stores teacher observations/test findings separately from disability information and supplied clinical notes. Teacher assessments record the date, encrypted observations, and the teacher who entered them. Disability information uses the existing category catalogue; diagnosis date, professional/clinic and clinical notes are optional and encrypted. Neither record approves a diagnosis, readiness, promotion or mainstreaming; attachments are handled by the document workflow.
- **School groupings**: Level 1 means 2 to under 3 years studied in the school; Level 2 means 3 through 4 years, including exactly 3 and exactly 4; Level 3 means more than 4 through 6 years. Transition Program means ages 15–21. These owner-confirmed school groupings are separate from graded levels. Year-counting rules remain unresolved; never assign or promote automatically. See `docs/CURRICULUM_REPORT_REFERENCE.md`.
- **Reporting periods**: SF9 indicator columns 1–3 mean Term 1, Term 2, and Term 3. Use exactly three teacher-remarks/parent-signature rows with those labels, with no fourth row. Teacher remarks and a parent signature are required every term; preserve the rest of the supplied Annex format. Plan periods per school year, with three terms for SY 2026–2027 and historical periods preserved. Dates and signature recording require confirmation.
- **Non-graded to graded**: needs the SPED assessment form and Schools Division Office (SDO) approval. Tracked in `learner_level_changes`. The tool records evidence and decisions. It never decides readiness.
- **Health and nutrition**: height and weight at baseline and endline each school year. BMI is calculated. Nutritional status and height-for-age categories use WHO growth standards by age and sex, computed in the app. A nurse or clinic confirms them. DepEd reports this on SF8.
- **Multi-school ready**: all school-owned data carries `school_id`. Another school can adopt the tool without seeing your data.

## 4. Build order

### Phase 1: Foundation
- Auth, roles, school, school year, sections (non-graded class)
- Learner profile (generated SMILE ID, optional LRN, disability, guardians, emergency contact, placement)
- Enrollment per school year (default non-graded)
- Document checklist per learner (required, submitted, verified, missing)
- Audit log and encryption of sensitive fields

Done when: the teacher can enroll learners, see the roster, and see missing documents.

### Phase 2: Core value
- Use the owner-supplied local `IEP SAMPLE.docx` as a working reference used by one school, **not the current approved IEP form**. See `docs/IEP_REFERENCE.md` for its structure, proposed improvements, schema gaps, and questions. Propose a blank/anonymized improved draft and confirm the final template, domain choices and wording with the owner/school before implementation. Do not label the draft DepEd-approved.
- IEP builder (baseline, goals, objectives, accommodations, services)
- Progress logging (not started, emerging, developing, mastered) with note and optional evidence
- Learner progress timeline and trend view
- IEP review dates and a "needs attention" list

Done when: the teacher can see each learner's progress and what to do next.

### Phase 3: Daily management and reports
- Use the owner-supplied non-graded SF1/SF2 spreadsheet formats as the report basis; see `docs/SF1_SF2_REFERENCE.md`. Resolve calendar, attendance-code, summary, and layout questions before implementation. Use blank/anonymized templates and fake test data.
- Daily attendance and the SF2 report
- Behavior and incident log
- Health and nutrition (height, weight, BMI, baseline and endline) and the SF8 report
- Printable reports: learner progress, IEP summary, class progress summary, missing documents
- Excel export of the class list for matching with LIS

Done when: the teacher can produce the reports DepEd and the school ask for without retyping.

**Phases 1 to 3 are the first usable release. Test with one teacher before building more.**

### Phase 4: Transitions and approvals
- Separate learner-specific Individualized Transition Plan (ITP), informed by the supplied transition curriculum; not a replacement for the IEP. Confirm its fields and approval workflow before implementation.
- Non-graded to graded workflow (SPED assessment form, SDO status, LIS updated)
- Mainstreaming transition plan (gradual hours, accommodations, review dates, path back to SPED)
- Level-change packet for the SDO
- Consent records

### Phase 5: Parents and wider use
- Parent portal (own child only)
- Reminders by email or SMS
- Vision, hearing, and dental screenings and referrals
- SF9 for non-graded learners using the owner-approved Annex basis adapted to exactly three term rows, with teacher remarks and required parent signature every term. SF10 after its format is confirmed. See `docs/CURRICULUM_REPORT_REFERENCE.md`.

### Phase 6: Later
- Multi-school onboarding and per-school settings (headers, logos, local document rules)
- Transfer flow between adopting schools, using the LRN
- Offline mode (PWA)
- Filipino/English interface
- Principal and division analytics
- Assessment and rubric library

## 5. Required documents (tracked by the tool)

These are checklist/follow-up requirements, not blanket prerequisites for non-graded enrollment. The owner permits later submission before school-year end, subject to teacher acceptance. Consent itself must be obtained before data collection; its signed copy may follow. Approved graded-level changes retain their assessment/SDO requirements.

**Enrollment, from parents:** enrollment form, PSA birth certificate, previous report card or SF9/SF10 (transferees), ID photos if required, consent forms (enrollment, data privacy, media, services)

**Enrollment, disability-specific:** medical or diagnostic report from a qualified professional, therapy records, PWD ID (optional), medication and health info, prior IEP

**Enrollment, from the school:** LIS record and LRN, SPED assessment form, initial screening, new or updated IEP, placement decision, baseline height and weight

**Moving to a regular class, from parents:** written request, signed consent to the transition plan, updated assessment if asked

**Moving to a regular class, from the school:** updated IEP or progress summary, SPED teacher recommendation, IEP team meeting record, transition plan, notice to the receiving teacher, follow-up review after 1 to 2 quarters, SDO approval for non-graded to graded

## 6. Reports

| Report | Source | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| SF2 Daily Attendance | `attendance`, enrollment history, class-day calendar | Monthly; supplied non-graded format, daily/monthly totals and summary calculations. See docs/SF1_SF2_REFERENCE.md. |
| SF8 Health and Nutrition | `health_measurements` | Baseline and endline |
| SF1 / class list export | learners, guardians, enrollments | Supplied non-graded School Register format; LIS owns the official record. See docs/SF1_SF2_REFERENCE.md. |
| SF9 | report-card assessments and attendance | Approved Annex basis adapted to exactly three term rows: Terms 1–3, teacher remarks and parent signature each term, separate P/AP/D/B/NO/NA scale. No fourth row. |
| SF10 | confirmed report data | Non-graded template still unconfirmed. |
| IEP summary and progress report | IEP tables | Printable for parents by school-year reporting period; Terms 1–3 for SY 2026–2027. Keep IEP ratings separate from SF9 ratings. |
| Learner progress report | `progress_entries` | Charts plus teacher notes |
| Class progress summary | `progress_entries` | Counts by rating per domain |
| Missing documents | `learner_documents` | Per section |
| Level-change packet | `learner_level_changes` | For SDO submission |

All reports: print-friendly PDF, plus Excel export where DepEd collects spreadsheets.

## 8. Security and privacy

Learner data is sensitive: medical and disability records of minors, under the Data Privacy Act (RA 10173).

- Every query scoped by `school_id`, and by section for advisers
- Policies on every model. Parents see only their own child.
- Audit log for views, edits, and exports of learner records
- Phase 1 audit-history access (owner-confirmed): the teacher/admin sees only events for learners in their own advised sections. Require school_admin plus sped_teacher; no school-wide metadata bypass. Keep history read-only and audit its viewing.
- Encrypt health and diagnosis fields
- Parental consent recorded before data collection
- Phase 1 records the teacher's confirmation that parental consent has already been obtained; the signed copy may be submitted later and tracked in the document checklist. Phase 4's broader transition consent workflow remains later work.
- HTTPS only, private file storage, daily backups
- Check hosting rules with the school or division before choosing a host

## 9. Acceptance checks (authorization)

These tests must exist and pass before any release:
1. An adviser cannot read or edit a learner in another adviser's section.
2. A user from school A cannot read any data from school B.
3. A parent can read only their own child's records.
4. `system_admin` cannot open learner records without an audit entry.
5. A non-graded learner cannot be marked graded without an approved level change.

## 10. Open questions (confirm with the school or division)

- Attachment replacement/deletion permissions, retention periods and whether prior versions must be retained. Initial Phase 1 uploads preserve the first attachment and do not silently replace or purge it.

- Reconcile the 17 document types in `schema.sql` with section 5 (photos, therapy records, medication information, LIS/screening/IEP and additional transition documents). Confirm which apply per learner/school; optional and conditional items are not blanket requirements. The first checklist uses the schema catalogue without enrollment blocking or automatic expiry.

- SF1/SF2 current version and cutoffs, applicable learner fields, attendance symbols/counts, class-day calendar, historical enrollment summaries, rounding, and print layout; see `docs/SF1_SF2_REFERENCE.md`.

- Final IEP template acceptance, annual review label, approval/amendment workflow, applicable domains/objectives, service schedule, and participants/signatures; the supplied sample is confirmed as a non-approved school reference. See `docs/IEP_REFERENCE.md`.
- How years studied are counted and Transition Program placement; Level 2 includes exactly three and four years. See `docs/CURRICULUM_REPORT_REFERENCE.md`.
- SF9 parent signature recording, assessment With/Without meaning, NO/NA handling, and attendance layout; its three term rows are confirmed. SF10 template remains unconfirmed.
- Separate ITP fields, review, approvals, and signatures.
- Whether LIS offers any integration for outside tools (assume manual export or import)
- Who may see which records (principal, regular teachers, therapists)
- Parent access method: email, mobile number, or in person
- Internet reliability at school (decides when offline mode moves up)
- Data hosting rules from the school or division
